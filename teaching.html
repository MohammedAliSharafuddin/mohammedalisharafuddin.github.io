My Own Courses

Two courses of my own, one free and self-paced, one paid and credit-bearing. Kept distinct rather than blurred together.

Maritime Clusters and International Digital Marketing
CMU MOOC free self-paced (~2 hours)

An overview of the Blue Economy, its sectors and clusters, and the fundamentals of international digital marketing: segmentation, sales and marketing funnels, social media, email, influencer, and mobile marketing. Certificate awarded at 70% or higher on the post-test.

Open course
The Art & Science of Digital Marketing
School of Lifelong Education, Chiang Mai University paid, credit-bearing (3 credits, 45 hours, 8 weeks, hybrid)

A full digital marketing curriculum: customer analysis, strategy, SEO, PPC, social and content marketing, email, video, influencer and affiliate marketing, marketing automation, and performance measurement. Delivered in English via Zoom and on-site sessions. 2023 (Thai calendar 2566) offering, application period closed.
